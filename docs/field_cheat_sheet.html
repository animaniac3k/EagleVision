SPOTR™ — RANGE FIELD CHEAT SHEET

RELEASE v0.62.2 • ANDROID vCode 64 • OFFLINE PWA
LAMINATE & KEEP IN RANGE BAG

1. 30-Second Range Setup

  • USB UVC Scope: Plug in USB-C/OTG cable → accept Android USB prompt → select camera in Setup → Camera.
  • Bresser Wi-Fi Scope: Join camera Wi-Fi SSID → open Setup → Camera → Wi-Fi / RTSP → tap Wake & Connect (192.168.0.1). Android 10+ auto-binds local Wi-Fi even with LTE on.
  • Auto-Reconnect: Stream recovers automatically with 4-step exponential backoff if Wi-Fi momentarily dips.
  • Zero & Calibrate: Tap ⚙ next to Hits (or long-press Hits) → tap ✦ Auto-Tune.

2. Live Screen Controls

  • Top-Left Pills: ⚙ Setup · 👁 Clean View (hides controls & reticle; both pills remain reachable).
  • Reticle · FFP: Toggle crosshair; tap FFP pill to lock subtensions 1:1 with zoom.
  • Hits | 🗑 | ⚙: Toggle live pins; 🗑 Clears & Re-Zeros immediately; ⚙ opens Calibration.
  • Voice: Cycles OFF → POI → POA (Cartesian clicks + clock bearings).
  • Loupe: Cycles ON → AA (Auto Aim) → OFF. Drag corners to resize.
  • Replay · 20s ▴: Opens unsaved LIVE BUFFER; tap 20s ▴ for duration/RAM meter.

  • Ignore Zones: Draw boxes over flapping tape, range flags, or adjacent targets.

4. Playback & Hits Map (Screen 2)

  • Playback Tab: Scrub audio-peak track, tap 0.5x slow-mo or -1 / +1 frame step. Tap Save Clip only for shots worth keeping; Export saves .webm.
  • Hits Tab: Reticle: ON/OFF and Hits: ON/OFF stay synchronized with Live; toggle 🕒 for hit timestamps.
  • Re-Zero / Clear (🗑): 1-tap Re-Zero or Clear hits; tap 📷 to export annotated PNG snapshot.

6. Field Troubleshooting

  • Wi-Fi Camera Won't Wake: Confirm Wi-Fi SSID connected, verify 192.168.0.1, tap Wake & Connect again (auto-retries 4x on drop).
  • Accidental Taps / Clean View: Tap top-left 👁 Clean View to hide bottom controls & reticle.
  • iOS / iPad RTSP: Run start_spotr.bat --lan on Windows laptop & open laptop LAN IP on iPad Safari.
