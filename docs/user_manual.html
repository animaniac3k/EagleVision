HARDWARE UVC Optical Scopes & Camera Input

SPOTR interfaces directly with standard USB Video Class (UVC) digital scopes, optical adapters, and webcams via the native HTML5 MediaDevices API.

  • Direct Plug & Play: Hot-plugging a scope triggers automatic stream renegotiation without page refreshes.
  • Performance Profiles: 1080p60 (recommended for scopes), 720p60 (low battery), or custom native capture.
  • Synthetic Test Signal: If no camera is connected, SPOTR launches a synthetic test pattern card with calibrated hash lines to verify compositor and reticle settings.
Lens & Aspect Ratio Engine

Canvas composition dynamically calculates letterbox/pillarbox geometry to preserve 1:1 true optical aspect ratios regardless of tablet display proportions.

INPUT BUS DualShock 4 Wireless Controller & Virtual Mouse

SPOTR features direct integration with Sony DualShock 4 controllers over Bluetooth utilizing the high-speed WebHID API (Report ID 0x11).

  • Touchpad Virtual Mouse: Clicking the center touchpad toggles the high-contrast blue Virtual Mouse cursor. Gliding a finger smoothly moves the pointer across all toolbars and sliders.
  • Tactical Triggers (L2 / R2):
    • L2 Trigger: Left Mouse Click & Drag (selects buttons, scrubs timelines, drags resizable PiP loupe).
    • R2 Trigger: Right Mouse Context Click.
  • Precision Joysticks: Left Stick controls primary scope reticle aim with a calibrated 50% precision rate curve. Right Stick steers the digital PiP zoom window.

DETECTION Thermal Micro-Change Heatmap Engine

SPOTR features an advanced, low-latency micro-movement detection algorithm that highlights subtle changes (bullet impacts, target movement, wind drift) as a colored thermal glow overlay.

  • Downsampled Luma Analysis: Downsamples the 1080p stream to a high-speed 160×90 luminance matrix at 60 FPS, calculating delta variance against an exponential moving average background.
  • Global Shift Rejection (>18%): Automatically discards whole-frame camera movements (wind buffeting, scope recoil, tripod bumps) to prevent false alerts.
  • Adjustable Sensitivity (10% to 95%): Fine-tune detection thresholds in Settings to spot minute paper tears at 1000 yards without triggering on heat mirage.
  • Decay Duration (0.5s to 10s): Controls how long the visual impact heat bloom persists before fading.
SPEECH Directional Voice Announcer (speechEngine.js)

When target movement or bullet impact is detected, SPOTR computes the geometric centroid vector relative to your scope crosshair and speaks the direction out loud using the browser's Web Speech API.

  • Directional Speech Cues: Announces target quadrant (e.g. "Target High Right", "Movement Low Left") or exact clock coordinates (e.g. "Impact 2 o'clock").
  • Customizable Keyword Dictionary: In Settings, operators can replace standard directional words with custom military or tactical keywords (e.g., replace "High" with "North" or "Elevation").
  • Intelligent Throttling: An internal speech queue and rate-limiter prevents audio cascades and ensures only fresh detections are spoken.
  • Immediate Voice Mute: Click the speaker icon in the top-left toolbar or press the Mute hotkey to instantly silence audio without disabling visual heatmap rendering.

IN-MEMORY Native-Resolution Circular RAM Ring Buffer

Traditional video recording causes tablet thermal throttling and flash storage wear. SPOTR solves this by maintaining a 100% RAM-only rolling queue (memoryRingBuffer.js) with zero disk writes during surveillance.

  • Configurable Duration: Store from 5 to 60 seconds of rolling historical video.
  • Configurable Buffer FPS: Set buffer capture between 5 and 30 FPS to optimize RAM footprint on low-memory devices (e.g., Chromebook Duet 3 with 4GB RAM).
  • Live RAM Budget Meter: Real-time telemetric bar in Settings displays Current Used MB, Estimated Allocation, and Free Device RAM.
  • Automatic Memory Guardrails: Proactively purges older frames if device memory pressure exceeds threshold, guaranteeing rock-solid stability without browser crashes.
PLAYBACK Instant Replay Viewport & Scrubbing

Capture critical shot strings instantly with a single button press or ring tap.

  • Instant Snapshot: Press Circle on controller, double-tap the center ring button, or click REPLAY to freeze and inspect the circular buffer.
  • Automatic Silent Persistence: Upon trigger, the snapshot is automatically cloned to permanent local IndexedDB storage in the background without stuttering playback.
  • Precision Timeline Scrubber: Drag the timeline slider to locate the exact microsecond of bullet impact or target change.
  • Slow-Motion & Frame Step: Play at 0.25x, 0.5x, or step frame-by-frame forward (STEP +1) and backward (STEP -1) for forensic ballistic review.
STORAGE Video Archive Library & High-Speed WebM Exporter

1. IndexedDB Local Storage

Clips are stored securely in browser sandboxed storage. Clips remain accessible across browser restarts and offline field sessions without cloud connectivity.

2. Fullscreen Analysis Player

Review saved clips in the full-screen Gallery Player. Features a collapsible side drawer with clip timestamps, frame counts, and duration badges.

3. One-Click WebM Export

The native export engine encodes recorded frames into a standard .webm video file, saving it directly to local disk for reporting, archiving, or sharing.

CALIBRATION MOA & MRAD Turret Click Calculator

Eliminate sighting guesswork by calculating exact optical turret click offsets directly from observed bullet impacts:

  • Target Distance Input: Enter engagement distance in Yards or Meters.
  • Turret Value Selection: Select your scope turret click standard (1/4 MOA, 1/8 MOA, 1/2 MOA, 0.1 MRAD, or 0.05 MRAD).
  • Point of Impact (POI) vs Point of Aim (POA): Measure target offset in millimeters or inches on the digital display.
  • Instant Turret Solution: SPOTR calculates exact turret clicks (e.g. "Elevation: +6 Clicks UP | Windage: -4 Clicks LEFT") to achieve pinpoint zero.
